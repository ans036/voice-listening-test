Listening study

Which voice sounds more natural?

Listen to two short recordings of the same sentence and pick the one that sounds more like a real person. You do this 34 times. There are no right or wrong answers: go with your first impression.

TimeAbout 15–20 minutes
Parts3 short parts: normal speed (~8 min), 1.5× faster (~4 min), 2× faster (~3 min), with a break between parts
SetupHeadphones or earphones in a quiet place, if you can
About your answers and privacy
  • Some recordings are computer voices and some are a real person. You are not told which is which.
  • Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time; nothing is sent until you finish.
  • We ask a few questions about you (age band, gender, hearing, how you are listening). We never ask your name or contact details.
  • Your answers are sent automatically when you finish and are reported only as group results in research on speech synthesis.

So that each person takes part only once, please answer three quick questions. Your browser turns the answers into a scrambled anonymous ID; your answers themselves are never sent or stored.
